## Before you upgrade

- 0.4.0 can't transfer with 0.3.0 or older. Update both computers, and any relay you run yourself.
- `drop recv` in a script now needs `--yes`. Without a terminal to ask, it refuses to start.
- The browser client is gone. Drop is the command-line tool.

## Added

- Windows: `drop.exe` for x86_64, installed with `install.ps1`. Transfers work between Windows, macOS and Linux.
- The receiver sees the name, type and size of what's coming, and nothing is saved until they accept.
- Ctrl-C on either side cancels the transfer and tells the other side.
- The sender sees what the receiver is doing: connecting, deciding, receiving, finishing.
- Separate exit codes for declined, cancelled here and cancelled by the other side. See `drop --help`.

## Fixed

- Someone guessing codes could hide their wrong guesses from the sender. The receiver now has to prove it has the right code before anything is sent.
- A stray connection attempt could end a direct transfer.
- Filenames and messages from the other side could change how your terminal behaves.
- A cancelled or broken transfer could leave a half-received file behind.
- Names Windows can't store, like `CON` or `notes:v2.txt`, are saved with `_` in their place, and the receiver is told.
